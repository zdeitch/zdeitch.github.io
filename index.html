∿2026 — PRESENTStudent Researcher · InterpretabilityDickinson College · DNU LabTraced how transformers find patterns in time-series data, then tested the mechanism.+

In my transformer interpretability research, I studied 4-, 6-, and 7-layer attention-only transformers to understand how they recover an unknown time lag.

I built a framework for tracing information through attention layers and derived an exact algebraic account of their scores. The analysis separates how early layers represent past values from how the last layer selects a predictive offset.

I tested the explanation with activation patching, ablation, sign changes, feature transplantation, and distribution shifts. Controlling final-layer attention reaches the oracle error floor at the true lag. A paper is in preparation.

PyTorchTransformerLensLinear algebra

Result: mean optimality gap fell from 1.15% to 1.00%. I’m also building a neural classifier to select effective formulations for binary quadratic programs.
